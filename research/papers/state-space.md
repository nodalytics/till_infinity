"Yeah. So basically I built it on the same principle as particle physics. Where by we don’t know where a particle is going to be unless you collapse the wave so all we have is a probability of the particle being in each position.

Now it picks the one with the highest probability and trade it at first it trades once a zone has a probability of 1 meaning certainty. 

It does have inputs, it takes in things like how fast the price moves(how much each tick pushed price up or down), how directional is the motion (that’s where directional efficiency comes from), how large is the liquidity zone, how close the price is to the zone. It channels everything through a function and then I use the weights to manually tune the effects of each property on the zone filling probability."
